How gold loan valuation works

From walk-in to documentation, in four steps

Bring your gold

Visit our New Sangvi showroom with the jewellery or gold you'd like valued.

Certified valuation

Our government-approved valuer assesses purity and weight on the spot.

Documentation

Bank submission

Take the certificate to any of our 10+ partner banks across 40+ branches.
